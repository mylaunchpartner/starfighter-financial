Fee Anatomy

Every processing fee has three parts

Bundled pricing hides which part is which. Once you can see the split, you can tell the difference between a cost and a markup.

Part 01 · Not ours

Interchange

Set by the card networks and paid to the card-issuing bank. Every processor pays the same rates. Nobody can discount this — anyone who says otherwise is repackaging something else.

Part 02 · Not ours

Network Assessments

Visa, Mastercard, Discover, and Amex each charge their own fees on top of interchange. Also fixed, also identical across processors.

Part 03 · Ours

Our Markup

The only part that's actually negotiable, and the only part we get paid on. We state it plainly and it stays where we set it — no re-pricing, no creep.

TWO OF THE THREE ARE THE SAME EVERYWHERE. THE THIRD IS WHERE PROCESSORS COMPETE — OR HIDE.
